Overview

About the Project

The Taadeen Platform is Saudi Arabia's unified mining licensing and investment platform, managed by ESNAD (Saudi Mining Services Company) — the executive arm of the Ministry of Industry and Mineral Resources. Launched as part of Saudi Vision 2030 to diversify the Kingdom's economy beyond oil, Taadeen facilitates the full investor journey: discovering available mining sites, issuing and renewing mineral licences, integrating with government e-systems, and providing geospatial mining data. This project involved developing key platform features using OutSystems as part of Link Development's delivery team.

Features

Key Features

  • Mineral Licence ManagementEnd-to-end digital workflows for issuing, renewing, and managing mining licences — replacing paper-based processes with a streamlined e-service.
  • Mining Site DiscoveryInteractive geospatial tools allowing investors to explore available mining sites across Saudi Arabia with detailed geological and regulatory information.
  • Investor PortalDedicated investor journey — from registration and site selection through licence application, compliance tracking, and renewal.
  • Government System IntegrationReal-time integration with Saudi government e-services including Nafath identity, payment gateways, and Ministry of Industry systems.
  • Compliance & ReportingRegulatory compliance monitoring and reporting tools for ESNAD staff and the Ministry to oversee mining sector activity.
Challenges

Technical Challenges

Geospatial Integration

Integrating GIS-based mining site data with the OutSystems platform required custom API bridges and careful data modelling to represent complex geological information accurately.

Multi-system Government Integration

Connecting with multiple Saudi government platforms — identity, payments, regulatory systems — each with different protocols and compliance requirements.

Regulatory Complexity

Encoding complex Saudi mining regulations and licence conditions into digital workflows required close collaboration with the Ministry's legal and technical teams.
