Overview

About the Project

The General Authority of Civil Aviation (GACA) is Saudi Arabia's official civil aviation regulator, overseeing all aspects of the Kingdom's aviation sector under Vision 2030's ambitious aviation transformation strategy — which targets making Saudi Arabia one of the world's top 10 aviation markets. This project involved developing and enhancing GACA's digital platform using OutSystems, delivering e-services for aviation licensing, permits, regulatory compliance, and stakeholder management across airlines, airports, and aviation businesses.

My Role

Responsibilities

As Software Engineering Specialist, I developed key modules on the OutSystems platform — implementing licensing workflows, permit management systems, and integrations with Saudi government digital infrastructure. I collaborated with GACA's technical team to align deliverables with regulatory requirements, led code reviews, managed debugging pipelines, and ensured all features met the authority's strict security and compliance standards.

Features

Key Features

  • Aviation Licensing PortalEnd-to-end digital workflows for issuing and renewing aviation licences — for airlines, airports, ground handlers, and aviation service providers.
  • Permit ManagementOnline system for managing flight permits, airspace authorisations, and special operations approvals with real-time status tracking.
  • Stakeholder E-ServicesSelf-service portal for aviation businesses to submit applications, track progress, and receive notifications — reducing manual processing time significantly.
  • Regulatory Compliance TrackingDashboards and audit tools enabling GACA inspectors to monitor compliance across regulated entities.
  • Government System IntegrationIntegrated with Saudi government identity (Nafath), payment gateways, and Ministry of Transport systems.
Challenges

Technical Challenges

Regulatory Complexity

Encoding Saudi civil aviation regulations and ICAO standards into digital workflows required deep collaboration with GACA's legal and operational teams to ensure accuracy.

Multi-system Integration

Connecting with Saudi government platforms — Nafath identity, payment systems, and inter-ministry data exchanges — each with distinct protocols and SLA requirements.

Performance at Scale

Ensuring the platform handled peak load during licence renewal periods — when hundreds of aviation entities submit simultaneously — required careful OutSystems performance tuning.
