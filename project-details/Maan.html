Overview

About the Project

The Authority of Social Contribution — Ma'an is Abu Dhabi's government body responsible for channelling social contributions toward impactful community projects. In 2024 alone, Ma'an allocated over AED 98.6 million across 51 projects spanning health, education, environment, and social care. This digital platform, built on OutSystems, provides a unified portal for contributors, social partners, and government stakeholders to discover projects, submit applications for funding, track deployment, and measure social impact.

My Role

Responsibilities

As Software Engineering Specialist, I developed key modules on the OutSystems platform — including contribution workflows, project tracking dashboards, and integration with Abu Dhabi government identity services. I collaborated closely with Ma'an's team to align features with their social investment process, coordinated API integrations with partner agencies, and ensured platform performance at scale during high-traffic funding cycles.

Key Features

  • Social Investment Fund PortalOnline portal for organisations to submit funding requests for social projects — with automated evaluation scoring and committee review workflows.
  • Contribution ManagementGovernment and private sector entities can register, manage, and track their social contributions across multiple projects and sectors.
  • Project DiscoveryPublic-facing directory of active social projects across health, education, environment, and infrastructure sectors seeking contributions.

Challenges

Technical Challenges

Multi-stakeholder Complexity

Supporting distinct user journeys for government agencies, private sector contributors, non-profit partners, and Ma'an administrators — each with different permissions and workflows.

Government System Integration

Integrating with Abu Dhabi government identity, payment, and reporting infrastructure required detailed API contracts and thorough compliance validation.

Impact Measurement

Designing data models and reporting dashboards that could accurately represent social impact across diverse project types and beneficiary groups.
